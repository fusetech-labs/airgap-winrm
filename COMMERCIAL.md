In short: if the servers are yours and the work is for your own organisation, you need nothing more than the
licence in this repository.

## When you need a commercial licence

Using the toolkit to deliver services to third parties is commercial use and needs a commercial licence from
Fusetech. Examples:

- a managed service provider patching, checking or auditing customers' servers with it;
- a consultancy using it on client engagements, including one-off audits;
- a reseller or integrator supplying it, or support for it, to customers;
- bundling it, or any part of it, in another product or service.

A commercial licence covers that use and comes with support terms agreed with you.
